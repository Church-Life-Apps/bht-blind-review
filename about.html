About this project

Rebuilding how BibleGo explains each verse

Every verse on BibleGo has a short help text drawn from classic commentaries. We rebuilt the way those texts are written, and your votes tell us whether readers agree it's better.

Best read after you've voted, since it describes how the new texts are written.

How a new text is written

An AI drafts. Plain rules and a fixed score decide. No AI judges quality.

  1. Gather the commentariesQuotes are chosen to represent each commentator in their own words, not just where they agree.
  2. Write about five drafts1,542 drafts for the 300 test verses.
  3. Throw out drafts that break a ruleThe rules caught 283 over-long sentences, 125 scholar names and 173 verse references before any reader saw them.
  4. Keep the draft most faithful to the commentariesTrusted commentaries count more. A new text replaces today's only if it scores clearly higher, by a margin fixed before testing.
  5. Ask real readersThat's this review.
