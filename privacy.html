Privacy

This page collects reviewer votes on BibleGo help texts. When you sign in, we receive your email address and name from Google or from the email sign-in link. We use them only to keep each person to one vote per verse.

We store your email address, your vote on each verse, any reason you write and the time you voted. The data is kept in a Supabase database. We use it only to compare the help texts and improve BibleGo. We do not sell it, share it for advertising or use it to contact you.

To have your votes and account deleted, contact the person who invited you to review.
